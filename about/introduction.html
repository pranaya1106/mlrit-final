About MLRIT

Since 2005, MLRIT has grown into a campus where learning extends far beyond the classroom. Students here are encouraged to build, question, compete, collaborate, and evolve — through academics, practical experience, innovation, and the culture around them.

Located in Dundigal, Hyderabad, and autonomous since 2015, the Institute is built on a simple belief: how students learn matters just as much as what they learn. That belief shapes everything here.

From core engineering to emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, Aeronautical and Aerospace Engineering, MLRIT combines academic foundations with industry relevance and hands-on exposure. Affiliated to JNTUH, the institution continues to create an environment that prepares students not only for careers, but for a rapidly changing world beyond them.

But what truly defines the MLRIT experience cannot be measured only through classrooms or curriculum. It is found in the confidence built over time, the challenges solved together, the discipline shaped through sports and teamwork, and the people who become part of your journey along the way.

Because education was never meant to stop at placements. It was meant to prepare you for everything that comes after.

What Defines MLRIT

Most colleges speak about holistic education. Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology was built to demonstrate what that truly means.

Since 2005, the Institute has believed that education should extend beyond classrooms, examinations, and transcripts. The goal has never been limited to producing graduates, but to shape individuals who can think independently, solve confidently, collaborate effectively, and adapt to a world that evolves faster than any syllabus.

That philosophy reflects across the institution. From core engineering disciplines to emerging fields such as Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Data Science, Aeronautical Engineering, and Aerospace Engineering, the academic structure is designed to balance strong fundamentals with practical relevance.

Autonomous since 2015 and affiliated to JNTUH, MLRIT has built an ecosystem where academics, innovation, employability, research, and sports are not treated as isolated achievements, but as connected parts of a meaningful education.

Located in Dundigal, on the edge of Hyderabad, the Institute continues to grow with the same clarity it began with: preparing students not only for careers, but for responsibility, change, and the ability to contribute wherever they go.

Academics. Innovation. Employability. Sports.
Not as separate priorities. As one philosophy.
