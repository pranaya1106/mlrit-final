Two Decades of Excellence

Since its founding in 2005, Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology has built a legacy of educational excellence, social impact, and institutional integrity. What began as a vision to provide world-class engineering education in Hyderabad has grown into an institution that has shaped thousands of careers, produced research of national significance, and earned the trust of students, families, and industry partners alike.

The legacy of MLRIT is written in the achievements of its alumni, the dedication of its faculty, and the relentless pursuit of quality that has defined the institution from day one.

Milestones

2005
Foundation

Marri Laxman Reddy Institute of Technology is established by the KMR Educational Society in Dundigal, Hyderabad. The institute opens with four engineering departments and an inaugural batch of students eager to learn in a brand-new, purpose-built campus.

2007
IQAC Established

The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) is constituted in line with NAAC guidelines, signalling MLRIT's commitment to institutionalised quality from an early stage.

2010
First NBA Accreditation

MLRIT achieves NBA accreditation for its flagship CSE programme, marking the first formal national recognition of academic quality and outcome-based education at the institute.

2015
UGC Autonomous Status

The University Grants Commission grants autonomous status to MLRIT — a landmark recognition that empowers the institution to design its own curricula, conduct examinations, and adapt rapidly to industry and societal needs.

2018
NAAC Grade A Accreditation

MLRIT is accredited by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council with Grade A — a testament to the institution's holistic academic environment, infrastructure, faculty quality, and student outcomes.

2020
Research Centres & IPFC

Multiple dedicated research centres are established across departments, and the Innovation, Product and Faculty Centre (IPFC) is launched to catalyse entrepreneurship, incubation, and applied research on campus.

2022
NAAC 2nd Cycle & Strategic Plan

MLRIT successfully completes its NAAC 2nd Cycle accreditation, retaining Grade A. A Strategic Perspective Plan 2022–27 is adopted, setting bold targets for research output, placement rates, and international collaborations.

2025
NIRF 201–300, Engineering

MLRIT appears in the NIRF 2025 rankings in the Engineering category (201–300), reflecting national recognition of its research, teaching quality, placement outcomes, and campus sustainability.

2026
Record Placement Season

The 2025–26 placement season closes with 621 offers from top-tier companies including Cognizant, Infosys, and HCL Tech, with the highest package at ₹51 LPA — the strongest recruitment season in MLRIT's history.

Governing Body

The Governing Body of MLRIT comprises distinguished academics, industry leaders, and senior administrators who provide strategic direction and ensure accountability. The body meets regularly to review institutional performance, approve policy changes, and chart the path forward for the institution.

Alumni Impact

MLRIT's greatest legacy is its alumni — engineers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and leaders who are making their mark across India and around the world. From Hyderabad's technology corridors to Silicon Valley, MLRIT graduates are recognised for their technical competence, work ethic, and values.

"MLRIT didn't just teach me engineering — it taught me how to think, how to lead, and how to serve. That foundation has been invaluable in everything I have done since."

Notably, in 2026, student Koduri Viplav was selected for the Viksit Bharat Young Leaders Dialogue — a national platform organised under the Prime Minister's office — reflecting the calibre and civic spirit of the MLRIT student community.
