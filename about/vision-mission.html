Our Vision & Mission

MLRIT's vision and mission form the philosophical foundation of everything we do — from curriculum design to campus culture. They guide our decisions, inspire our community, and define our commitment to students, society, and the nation.

V
Vision

Promote academic excellence, research, innovation, and entrepreneurial skills to produce graduates with human values and leadership qualities to serve the nation.

M
Mission

M1. Provide student-centric education and training on cutting-edge technologies to make the students globally competitive and socially responsible citizens.

M2. Create an environment to strengthen the research, innovation and entrepreneurship to solve societal problems.

Vision

"Promote academic excellence, research, innovation, and entrepreneurial skills to produce graduates with human values and leadership qualities to serve the nation."

The vision of MLRIT is built on four interconnected pillars: academic excellence, research and innovation, entrepreneurial skills, and human values and leadership. Every programme, policy, and initiative at the institute is evaluated against these pillars to ensure we remain true to our founding purpose.

Pillar
Academic Excellence

Rigorous curricula, outcome-based education, and continuous benchmarking against national and global standards.

Pillar
Research & Innovation

Dedicated research centres, sponsored projects, and a culture of curiosity that drives meaningful discovery.

Pillar
Entrepreneurial Skills

IPFC Centre, incubation support, and industry partnerships that help students build and launch ideas.

Pillar
Human Values & Leadership

Ethical education, social responsibility, and leadership development woven into every student's journey.

Mission

M1

"Provide student-centric education and training on cutting-edge technologies to make the students globally competitive and socially responsible citizens."

This mission drives MLRIT's curriculum design philosophy. We place the student at the centre of all learning decisions — choosing technologies, delivery methods, and assessments based on what best equips students for the future. Our autonomous status enables us to rapidly integrate emerging technologies such as AI, cloud computing, data science, and cybersecurity into our programmes.

M2

"Create an environment to strengthen the research, innovation and entrepreneurship to solve societal problems."

MLRIT believes that engineering education carries a responsibility to society. Research at MLRIT is deliberately oriented toward real-world problem solving — from sustainable infrastructure to healthcare technology. Through the IPFC Centre and collaborations with government agencies and industry, students and faculty tackle challenges that matter.

How We Live Our Mission

  • Outcome-Based Education (OBE) aligned with graduate attributes
  • Industry-integrated curriculum updated every academic year
  • Dedicated research budget and infrastructure support
  • MoUs with leading companies for internships and joint projects
  • Student entrepreneurship incubation through the IPFC Centre
  • NSS and NCC programmes fostering social responsibility
  • Global exposure through faculty development and exchange programmes

Quality Policy

MLRIT is committed to delivering education of the highest quality through continuous improvement, stakeholder engagement, and adherence to national and international standards. Our quality policy is operationalised through the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC), which monitors, evaluates, and drives improvement across all institutional processes.

"We shall impart quality technical education through well-qualified and dedicated faculty, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and industry-aligned curriculum — developing competent, ethical, and socially aware engineers."
