MLRIT Prospectus 2025–26

The official MLRIT prospectus provides a comprehensive overview of the institute's programmes, academic calendar, admission procedures, fee structure, scholarships, facilities, and campus life. It is the definitive guide for prospective students and their families.

The prospectus covers all undergraduate B.Tech programmes, postgraduate M.Tech and MBA programmes, and includes details on the MLRIT campus, hostel facilities, sports infrastructure, and the placement track record.
