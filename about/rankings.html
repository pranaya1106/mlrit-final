Accreditations

Accreditation from national bodies confirms that MLRIT meets rigorous criteria across academic quality, faculty credentials, infrastructure, research, governance, and student outcomes.

NAAC — National Assessment and Accreditation Council

MLRIT holds Grade A accreditation from NAAC (2nd Cycle). The NAAC assessment evaluates institutions across seven criteria: Curricular Aspects, Teaching-Learning and Evaluation, Research Innovation and Extension, Infrastructure and Learning Resources, Student Support and Progression, Governance Leadership and Management, and Institutional Values.

NBA — National Board of Accreditation

NBA accreditation covers individual programmes and is awarded to programmes that demonstrate strong outcomes in the context of graduate attributes and programme educational objectives.

  • Computer Science and Engineering — Cycle II
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering — Cycle II
  • Mechanical Engineering — Cycle II
  • Aeronautical Engineering — Cycle I
  • CSE (Data Science) — Cycle I
  • CSE (AI & ML) — Cycle I

AICTE — All India Council for Technical Education

All B.Tech and M.Tech programmes at MLRIT are approved by AICTE, the statutory authority for technical education in India. Annual compliance reports are submitted to AICTE and approvals are maintained continuously.
